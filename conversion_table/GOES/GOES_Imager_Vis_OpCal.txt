*: This post-launch calibration coefficient was derived from 
the GOES-15 and Terra MODIS collocation cloud pixels acquired 
on Y10D228, Y10D244, Y10D248, Y10D253, Y10D273, Y10D276, Y10D278, 
Y10D285, Y10D289, and Y10D294. The coefficient shown is the 
average coefficient for the Y10D228 to Y10D294 time period (the 
C = α*exp(bt) equation was not used).

**: This coefficient was calculated for the time period from Dec. 
6, 2011, when the satellite became operational as GOES-West, to 
Dec. 13, 2011, when the regular December update of the correction 
coefficient in GVAR occurs. It is optimal for Dec. 6th, 2011.

***: To prevent confusion, it has been decided that STAR will 
only publish correction coefficients that are the same or greater 
than the correction coefficient from the previous month. So, if 
the α and b values provided in the table are such that the 
resulting coefficient would be smaller than the previous month's, 
then the published coefficient will be the same as the previous 
month's. Note that this will cause the α and b values in those 
months to not yield the correction value shown. Please see this 
document for more information.

Please note that the GOES-15 was also drifted to 61.5°E on Oct. 
2023 and was renamed as EWS-G2. The coefficients from November 
2023 onward are derived from the NOAA-20 Visible Infrared 
Imaging Radiometer Suite (VIIRS).

(For the details of the methodology, please see: 
"Post-launch calibration of GOES Imager visible channel using 
MODIS" by X. Wu and S. Sun, 2005, SPIE, Vol. 5882, doi:10.1117/12.615401)
